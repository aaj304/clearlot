How it works

The sidewalk is an add-on to driveway clearing. When you book, pick your driveway size, then regular or corner lot sidewalk. We clear the stretch of sidewalk beside your property, edge to edge.

On the whole winter plan, your sidewalk is cleared after every snowfall of 5 cm or more, along with your driveway.
