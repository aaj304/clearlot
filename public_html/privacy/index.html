Privacy

When you book, we collect your address, your name, your phone number, and your email if you give it, plus anything you write in the notes. If you book for someone else, we also collect their name and phone number.

We use this only to confirm your booking, clear your snow and contact you about it. The crew doing the work sees your address and notes. We do not sell your information.

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, contact us.
