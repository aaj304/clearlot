Questions people ask

See all questions

How fast will you clear my snow?

For a one-time clearing, within 24 hours of your booking. On the whole winter plan, within 24 hours after each snowfall of 5 cm or more stops.

How do I know what size my driveway is?

Count how many cars fit side by side. One car wide is a single driveway, two is a double and three is a triple. If you also have a pad for an RV, trailer or boat beside the driveway, pick extra-long or RV pad.

Not sure? Pick the closest one. We check when we call to confirm.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged online. After you book, we call you to confirm the details and arrange payment.

What is a corner lot sidewalk?

If your home is on a corner, there is city sidewalk along the front and along the side of your lot. Pick corner lot sidewalk and we clear both.

What does the whole winter plan cover?

Every snowfall of 5 cm or more from November 1 to March 31, for the areas you picked. You pay one price for the winter, and it does not go up if it snows a lot. Pay it all at once, or in 5 monthly payments from November to March.
